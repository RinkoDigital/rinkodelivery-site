What's Different From A One-Off Order

Same drivers, same coverage area — different billing.

A B2B contract doesn't change how the delivery happens. It changes how it's priced and billed.

No card per delivery

One monthly invoice covers every delivery in that period, instead of a charge each time.

Rate tied to volume

Consistent, higher-volume routes get a lower per-delivery rate than one-off pricing.

Priority scheduling

Recurring routes get scheduled ahead of time instead of competing with same-day walk-in requests.
