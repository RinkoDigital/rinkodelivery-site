Delivery Guidelines

These guidelines help ensure smooth, safe, and reliable local deliveries with Rinko Delivery LLC.

Last Updated: May 5, 2026

Safety & Delivery Verification

For safety and proof of delivery, Rinko Delivery LLC may use delivery verification tools such as photos, GPS confirmation, or visible cameras when appropriate.

Our goal is to protect both customers and drivers while keeping deliveries smooth, transparent, and professional.

1. Parties

This Agreement is entered into by and between Rinko Delivery LLC, a Washington limited liability company (“Company,” “we,” “us,” or “our”), and the individual or business requesting delivery services (“Customer,” “you,” or “your”).

2. Services

Rinko Delivery LLC provides local courier and same-day delivery services, including pickup, transport, and drop-off of eligible items within our service area, including Bothell, Seattle, Bellevue, Kirkland, Redmond, Woodinville, and surrounding areas.

Unless otherwise agreed in writing, deliveries are performed using a standard passenger vehicle/sedan.

3. Delivery Guidelines

  • Items must safely fit inside a standard sedan.
  • No illegal, hazardous, flammable, explosive, perishable without notice, medical, controlled, restricted, or regulated items are accepted.
  • No furniture moving, heavy hauling, large appliance transport, or oversized cargo unless expressly accepted in writing.
  • Fragile, high-value, temperature-sensitive, or special-handling items must be declared before pickup.
  • We may refuse or cancel any order that appears unsafe, unlawful, oversized, improperly packaged, or outside our service scope.

4. Customer Responsibilities

  • Provide accurate pickup and drop-off addresses, contact information, access instructions, and item descriptions.
  • Ensure items are properly packed, sealed, labeled, and ready at pickup.
  • Ensure an authorized person is available at pickup and delivery when required.
  • Disclose any item value, fragile condition, special handling needs, or safety concerns before dispatch.

5. Pricing & Payment

Pricing may be based on distance, item size, delivery urgency, waiting time, tolls, parking, special handling, or other service conditions. Payment is due before or at the time of service unless otherwise agreed in writing.

Additional charges may apply for inaccurate information, excessive waiting, failed pickup/drop-off, re-delivery, special access, parking, tolls, or customer-requested changes.

6. Changes & Cancellations

Orders canceled after driver dispatch may be subject to a cancellation fee. If pickup or drop-off cannot be completed due to incorrect information, no access, unavailable recipient, unsafe conditions, or customer no-show, the order may be charged in full.

7. Delivery Times

Delivery times are estimates only and are not guaranteed. Rinko Delivery LLC is not responsible for delays caused by traffic, weather, road closures, accidents, customer delays, incorrect information, emergencies, or other events outside our control.

8. Delivery Confirmation

Proof of service may include photos, customer confirmation, recipient confirmation, GPS timestamps, delivery notes, body camera footage, dash camera footage, signature, or other reasonable documentation. Such documentation may be used to verify pickup, condition, delivery completion, location, timing, and dispute resolution.

9. Safety & Recording

To improve safety and delivery verification, we may use photos, GPS confirmation, dash cameras, or visible body cameras during deliveries when appropriate.

Any delivery documentation is used only for operational, safety, or dispute-resolution purposes and is not used publicly without permission.

Customers may request contactless delivery whenever available.

Private areas should not be recorded beyond what is reasonably necessary to confirm pickup or delivery. Customers may request contactless pickup/drop-off when available.

10. Service Responsibility

While we handle deliveries with care, Rinko Delivery LLC is not responsible for issues caused by improper packaging, incorrect information, unsafe conditions, or circumstances outside our control. For standard deliveries, responsibility is limited to $100 unless additional coverage is arranged before pickup.

We recommend notifying us in advance about fragile, valuable, or special-handling items.

11. Prohibited Items

Customer agrees not to tender illegal drugs, weapons, ammunition, explosives, hazardous materials, live animals, human remains, medical specimens, controlled substances, alcohol requiring special licensing, tobacco/nicotine products requiring special licensing, counterfeit goods, stolen property, or any item prohibited by applicable law or our policies.

12. Fair Use Policy

Customers are responsible for ensuring items are legal, safe to transport, and properly packaged before pickup.

13. Independent Contractor Status

Rinko Delivery LLC provides services as an independent business. Nothing in this Agreement creates an employment, partnership, joint venture, agency, or franchise relationship between Customer and Rinko Delivery LLC.

14. Governing Law & Dispute Resolution

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Washington. Any dispute shall be handled in a court or forum with proper jurisdiction in Washington, unless the parties agree to another lawful method of resolution.

15. Acceptance

By requesting a delivery, submitting a quote request, or completing payment, customers acknowledge these delivery guidelines.
